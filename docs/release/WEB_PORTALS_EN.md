- Controls: Mouse or touch
- Players: Single player

One-line description:

> Lift colorful bead groups, make room in a 20-slot tray, and rebuild 50 handcrafted pixel-art puzzles.

Short description:

> Beadscape is a relaxing spatial puzzle about moving whole clusters instead of tapping one bead at a time. Read each shape, lift a connected color group, use the tray as temporary space, and drop the group onto its matching pattern.

Long description:

> Every board in Beadscape begins as a colorful pattern that has been shuffled in broad connected groups. Your job is to move those groups back onto their matching spaces. The tray holds only 20 beads, while later groups can be larger, so a satisfying two-stage relay is sometimes the cleverest solution. Across 50 handcrafted levels, the boards grow from hearts, flowers and fish into tall rockets, winding trains, dinosaurs, cities and full-color mosaics. There is no timer and no move limit: experiment freely, enjoy the cascade of beads, and solve each picture at your own pace.

## Features

- Move large connected bead groups in a single action.
- 50 handcrafted portrait, landscape and rectangular boards.
- A 20-slot tray creates spatial choices without a timer or move limit.
- Local progress remembers the latest unlocked level and completed puzzles.
- Move counter lets players compare more efficient solutions.
- Automatic English / Chinese selection with a manual language switch.
- No account, payment, advertising, chat or user-generated content.

## How to play

1. Tap or click a connected group of matching beads to lift it.
2. Tap the empty tray to store the group, or tap a matching open area on the board.
3. Continue relaying groups until every bead matches its background color.

## Poki

Submit the playable prototype first. Do not add Poki SDK calls until the game is accepted. Poki is curated and asks for web exclusivity; that condition conflicts with publishing the same build publicly on itch.io and CrazyGames, so confirm any exclusivity terms before signing or enabling a Poki launch.

The current portrait-first layout is a reasonable prototype, but Poki's quality guide asks games to fill a 16:9 canvas across desktop, mobile and tablet. Treat submission as an application and expect a responsive-layout pass if Poki accepts the concept.

Official guidance: <https://sdk.poki.com/>, <https://developers.poki.com/guide/requirements-quality>, and <https://developers.poki.com/guide/how-testing-works>
